About

Building Uganda's Space Ecosystem

A community-driven platform connecting students, researchers, professionals, and organizations in Uganda's growing space sector.

The Uganda Space Community (USC) is a centralized platform built to unite everyone passionate about space science and technology in Uganda. Whether you are a university student exploring astrophysics, a researcher publishing satellite data, an engineer building CubeSats, or a policymaker shaping national space strategy — USC is where you connect, learn, and contribute.

We believe Uganda's space future is built by its people. By removing barriers to information, fostering collaboration across disciplines, and amplifying local achievements, we aim to accelerate the country's role in the global space economy.

What We Do

Discover

Find events, news, training programs, scholarships, and career opportunities across Uganda's space sector — all in one place.

Connect

Meet members, join organizations, and build relationships with institutions and professionals shaping Uganda's space future.

Contribute

Share knowledge through the community knowledge base, mentor students, and participate in programs that grow national capability.

Our Mission

To be the central hub for Uganda's space community — connecting people, sharing knowledge, and accelerating the country's space capabilities. We envision a Uganda where every aspiring space professional has access to the resources, network, and opportunities they need to thrive.

Who It's For

USC is open to anyone with an interest in space science, technology, and policy. Our community includes:

Students Researchers Engineers Policymakers Journalists Educators Organizations Enthusiasts
