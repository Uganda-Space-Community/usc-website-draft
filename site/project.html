National Astronomy Week

14–20 Jul 2026 · Nationwide

Celebrating Astronomy Across Uganda

National Astronomy Week is a week-long celebration of astronomy and space science held across Uganda. Now in its third year, the program brings together universities, observatories, schools, and community organizations to inspire the next generation of astronomers and space scientists.

The week features public stargazing events, school visits, lectures by leading astronomers, planetarium shows, and hands-on workshops. Events are held in Kampala, Entebbe, Gulu, Mbarara, and Jinja, making astronomy accessible to communities nationwide.

Schedule Highlights

  • Day 1: Opening ceremony at Makerere University with keynote by Dr. Sarah Nabukenya
  • Day 2: School outreach — astronomers visit 20 schools across central Uganda
  • Day 3: Public lecture: "Uganda's Role in African Astronomy" at Uganda Museum
  • Day 4: Telescope workshop at Entebbe Observatory — hands-on observation training
  • Day 5: Astro-photography competition and exhibition
  • Day 6: Regional events in Gulu, Mbarara, and Jinja
  • Day 7: Closing night — mass stargazing event at multiple locations

Get Involved

Volunteers are needed for school outreach, event coordination, and social media coverage. If you're a university student or astronomy enthusiast, sign up to help make this year's National Astronomy Week the biggest yet.
