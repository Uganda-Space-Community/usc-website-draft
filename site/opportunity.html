Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Full Funding

Full Scholarship for African Students

The African Space Agency is offering full scholarships for African students pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate degrees in space science, aerospace engineering, or related fields. This prestigious program covers tuition, living expenses, and research costs for the duration of the study program.

Selected scholars will have the opportunity to study at leading African universities and participate in research projects aligned with the African Space Agency's mission to advance space capabilities across the continent.

Eligibility

  • Citizen of an African Union member state
  • Admitted to or enrolled in an accredited university program in space science, aerospace engineering, physics, or related fields
  • Minimum GPA of 3.5 or equivalent
  • Demonstrated commitment to Africa's space development
  • Not currently receiving another full scholarship

What's Covered

  • Full tuition and fees
  • Monthly living stipend
  • Research and conference travel grants
  • Health insurance
  • Mentorship from African Space Agency professionals

How to Apply

Applications must include a completed application form, academic transcripts, two letters of recommendation, a personal statement of purpose, and a research proposal aligned with the agency's strategic priorities. Applications are reviewed by a committee of space science professionals.
