Community

Code of Conduct

Guidelines that help us maintain a respectful, inclusive, and productive community for everyone.

Respect & Inclusion

We are committed to creating a welcoming environment for all participants regardless of race, gender, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, or national origin. Every member of the Uganda Space Community deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.

Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind will not be tolerated. We encourage open dialogue and constructive disagreement, but always with professionalism and empathy. If someone expresses a viewpoint you disagree with, engage with the idea — not the person.

Knowledge Sharing

The strength of our community lies in the open exchange of knowledge. Share your expertise generously, cite sources when referencing others' work, and give credit where it is due. When posting in the knowledge base or forums, aim for clarity, accuracy, and accessibility.

Respect intellectual property. Do not present others' work as your own, and obtain permission before sharing proprietary information. When in doubt, ask. Our knowledge base thrives on trust and integrity.

Collaboration

Space science is inherently collaborative. Be open to working with people from different backgrounds, disciplines, and experience levels. Mentor those who are earlier in their journey, and remain humble enough to learn from those with different perspectives.

When collaborating on projects, communicate clearly, set expectations early, and follow through on commitments. Disagreements are natural — resolve them professionally and keep the focus on the shared goal.

Integrity

Act honestly in all your interactions within the community. Do not misrepresent your qualifications, fabricate data, or mislead others. Scientific integrity is the foundation of trust in our field, and we hold ourselves to the highest standards.

If you make an error, acknowledge it and correct it promptly. Transparency builds credibility, and credibility builds a stronger community.

Safety

The safety of our community members is paramount. Do not share personal information about others without consent. Do not engage in or encourage any activity that could endanger yourself or others, whether in physical events or online spaces.

Report any safety concerns immediately. We take all reports seriously and will investigate promptly and confidentially.

Report Issues

If you experience or witness a violation of this Code of Conduct, please report it confidentially. We are committed to investigating all reports fairly and taking appropriate action.
