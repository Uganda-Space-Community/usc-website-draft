Satellite Applications Workshop

12 Jul 2026 · Makerere University, Kampala · 09:00 – 17:00 EAT

About This Event

Join us for a full-day hands-on workshop on satellite data processing and Earth observation. Learn to work with Sentinel-2 imagery, perform land cover classification, and analyze environmental changes using open-source tools.

This workshop is designed for students, researchers, and professionals interested in remote sensing and geospatial analysis. No prior experience with satellite data is required — we'll cover everything from the basics.

What You'll Learn

Participants will gain practical skills in downloading and preprocessing Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, performing supervised and unsupervised land cover classification, and creating time-series analyses of environmental changes in Uganda.

The workshop uses Google Earth Engine and QGIS — both free and open-source platforms — so you can continue working with satellite data after the event.

Who Should Attend

This workshop is ideal for university students in geography, environmental science, agriculture, or engineering, as well as researchers and professionals working with spatial data. Government agencies and NGOs involved in land management and environmental monitoring will also benefit.
